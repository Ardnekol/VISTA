═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5 — FULL ANALYSIS & PAPER (month 3-4)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Paper title: "When Situation Overrides Values: LLM-Simulated
              Decision Making Under Contextual Pressure"

Core claim: Situational modifiers shift value-based decisions in LLMs,
            and this shift is moderated by profile strength —
            people with strong values resist situational pressure more
            than people with weak values.

Paper structure:
  1. Introduction     — values alone don't predict decisions, situation matters
  2. Related Work     — Schwartz values, moral stories, ValueActionLens
  3. Method           — profile encoding → scenario → modifier → LLM decision
  4. Experiment 1     — Llama vs Gemma4: modifier flip rates, model comparison
  5. Experiment 2     — Human study: do LLM predictions match real humans?
  6. Analysis         — which modifier types matter most, moderated by profile strength
  7. Conclusion       — LLMs can simulate value-contextual decision making

Ablations needed:
  - Profile only (no modifier) vs modifier only (no profile) vs both
  - Different temperature settings for LLM
  - Scenario domain effects (workplace vs community vs personal)
