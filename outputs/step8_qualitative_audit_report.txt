VISTA — STEP 8: QUALITATIVE AUDIT
========================================================================================

Plain-English question:
  When a strong-value LLM flips under a modifier, does its
  reasoning ACTUALLY cite the modifier? Or is it flipping for
  unrelated reasons?

Sample frame:
  profile_strength >= 4   (strong-value profiles)
  axis in {self_preservation, authority_signal}
  llm_changed_from_baseline == YES

Auto-coding signals:
  (1) axis_keyword_in_reasoning: reasoning contains at least one
      hand-curated axis keyword (e.g., 'danger', 'safety',
      'authority', 'official').
  (2) modifier_overlap_score: fraction of modifier_text content
      words that appear in the reasoning (>=0.3 = 'high overlap').

How to read:
  - %kw is the share of strong-profile flips where the LLM's
    reasoning mentions an axis keyword. High = the flip is
    explicitly modifier-driven, not noise.
  - %high_overlap is stricter: reasoning re-uses content words
    from the modifier_text itself.
