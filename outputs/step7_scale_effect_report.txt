----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TWO COMPETING INTERPRETATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. CAPABILITY STORY:
   Smaller models lack the capability to 'understand' the modifier
   and weave it into their reasoning. Sensitivity emerges as the
   model grows large enough to follow multi-step social reasoning.
   Predicts: per-axis flip rates rise monotonically with scale.

2. ALIGNMENT-SUPPRESSION STORY:
   Smaller models receive heavier RLHF for 'consistent, principled'
   answers. RLHF teaches them to ignore situational pressure and
   stick to their stated values. Large models retain more of the
   base-model social-psychology pull.
   Predicts: flip rate is suppressed at the small end, not absent.

   The two stories make similar predictions; our data cannot
   distinguish them with only 4 models. Honest path: acknowledge
   both, propose follow-up with base+instruct pairs at matched scale.

  that scale modulates the magnitude but not the structure of the effect."
