VISTA — STEP 4: LOGISTIC REGRESSION (PROFILE FE + SCENARIO FE + AXIS)
========================================================================================

Plain-English question:
  If we already know the scenario AND the person, does knowing
  the modifier ALSO help predict the LLM's decision?

Method:
  REDUCED model: y ~ C(person) + C(scenario)
  FULL    model: y ~ C(person) + C(scenario) + C(axis)
  Likelihood-ratio (LR) test on the axis terms.
  C(person) absorbs ALL profile-level info (each person gets
  their own baseline). Anything axis explains is on top of that.

How to read this:
  - LR_chi2 / p: if p is very small, the modifier term improves
    prediction beyond person + scenario. This is the formal
    statistical statement of the paper's claim.
  - dAIC > 0 (positive) means the FULL model wins on AIC.
    Rule of thumb: dAIC > 10 is 'strong' evidence.
  - dBIC > 10 is 'very strong' evidence (BIC penalises params more).
  - McFadden R² gain = how much extra variance the modifier explains.
